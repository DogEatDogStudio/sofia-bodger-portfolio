Case Studies

H&M Social Relaunch

H&M Social Strategy

Complete repositioning of H&M's social presence to rebuild brand love with a fashion-forward audience. The approach was bold enough that H&M wiped their Instagram entirely—generating organic press coverage before a single new post went live.

The Approach

Sofia led the strategy to relaunch H&M's entire social media presence from the ground up. This involved building a comprehensive social playbook that repositioned H&M's platforms through a culture-first lens, followed by the execution of culture-leading content across Instagram and TikTok.

The Outcome

The relaunch positioned H&M at the heart of culture conversations, driving brand love, earned media, and long-term credibility. The wipe moment alone generated significant organic press from fashion and culture publications.

Nike DFB Women's Team

Nike Campaign

Campaign to champion the DFB Women's Team ahead of the World Cup, shifting cultural attention from the men's game and positioning these players as icons in their own right.

The Approach

Sofia partnered with Nike to build a campaign that centred the players—Giulia Gwinn, Klara Bühl, and Selina Cerci—as cultural figures rewriting the rules of football. The strategic frame was about inspiration and name recognition: making the next generation know who these players are.

The Outcome

The campaign successfully shifted the narrative around women's football in the German market and aligned Nike with a progressive, culture-forward position in sport.

Nike Airtopia

Nike Digital

Air Max Day activation that spoke authentically to a new generation of kids—going beyond product to create a genuine cultural moment built around creativity and play.

The Approach

Sofia developed the strategy for Nike Airtopia: a magical world of Air floating above Nikeland in Roblox. The concept was built in collaboration with the Nike Play Council—a new generation of kid creatives—who transformed their physical creations into a digital world of wonder.

The Outcome

Nike Airtopia delivered a culturally resonant Air Max Day moment that connected digital and physical creativity, positioned Nike as genuinely youth-forward, and created a platform—the Play Council—with long-term strategic utility.

Nike Mind Sets

Nike Platform

A 365-day global platform leveraging the healing power of movement with a critical reframe—shifting the focus from 'doing' to 'feeling'.

The Approach

Sofia developed the strategy for Nike Mind Sets: a programme that rolled out across the Nike app, NRC, NTC, email, .com, social handles, and athlete channels. The strategy grounded mental wellbeing within Nike's existing movement ecosystem rather than launching a separate vertical.

The Outcome

Nike Mind Sets became a sustained, always-on programme that meaningfully extended Nike's cultural relevance into the mental health space—without feeling performative.

Nike FlyEase

Nike Cultural

Relaunch strategy for Nike's hands-free sneaker (Time Magazine's best invention of 2021) that got the shoe onto the feet of adaptive athletes who actually needed it.

The Approach

Sofia reframed Nike's 'Behind the Design' content format into 'Why The Design'—moving the narrative from aesthetic to purpose. Rather than just showing the innovation, the campaign showed who it was designed for and why that mattered.

The Outcome

The relaunch successfully repositioned FlyEase as a purposeful innovation for athletes of all abilities, rather than a hype object. The 'Why The Design' format gave Nike a repeatable creative framework for purpose-led product storytelling.

Foot Locker

Foot Locker Brand Platform

'Shoes Don't Change The World'—a brand platform positioning Foot Locker's $200M pledge to the Black community with cultural credibility, not corporate messaging.

The Approach

Sofia built the brand platform from the insight that shoes don't make the person—the person makes the shoes. The platform centred next-generation stories of progress: young people using culture, creativity, and self-expression to create genuine change.

The Outcome

The platform was adopted as Foot Locker's primary brand positioning across EU markets. Campaign content achieved nearly 10,000 likes per post on Instagram. Sofia presented the strategy at the Campaign Purpose Summit alongside the Foot Locker team.

Beats Daisy

Beats By Dre Digital Campaign

Beats' first campaign on TikTok establishing genuine cultural credibility with a young, creator-led audience using the launch of Powerbeats Pro.

The Approach

Sofia developed the strategy for the #BeatsDaisyChallenge: a campaign built around Ashnikko's new single 'Daisy'. Rather than a traditional advertising approach, the campaign invited audiences to become co-creators of Ashnikko's music video. Over four weeks, participants were challenged to create colour-themed TikToks inspired by the Powerbeats Pro.

The Outcome

8.1 billion views in the first month. 2.2 million video creations in 21 days. 600,000 unique creators uploading in the first week. 946 million engagements. Covered by Campaign, NME, Music Week, Adweek, The Verge, and others.

Johnnie Walker Blonde

Johnnie Walker Campaign

Campaign to reframe whisky for a younger, more diverse drinker who had historically dismissed whisky as not for them—without alienating existing drinkers.

The Approach

Sofia developed the strategy for a TV campaign that invited whisky-sceptics to 'take a walk on the bright side'. The tone was warm, aspirational, and deliberately upbeat—a direct contrast to the category's traditionally masculine, heritage-heavy positioning.

The Outcome

The Johnnie Walker Blonde campaign successfully introduced the product to a younger, more culturally diverse audience and gave the brand a credible entry point into a new consumer segment.

Audi Synchronised Swimming

Audi Campaign

Campaign to communicate that Audi's smaller models carry the same DNA and performance credentials as flagship vehicles—without positioning the A1 as a compromise.

The Insight

There is no greater demonstration of two entities matching each other perfectly than synchronised swimmers. The campaign swapped swimmers for an Audi A7 and an A1, performing mirror-image choreography in a competition pool.

The Outcome

The Audi Synchronised Swimming film delivered a distinctive, highly memorable creative execution that cut through category advertising norms and successfully reframed the A1 as a deliberate choice rather than an entry-level compromise.
