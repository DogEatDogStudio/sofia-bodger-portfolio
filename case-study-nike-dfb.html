Nike DFB Women's Team

Nike Campaign

Campaign to champion the DFB Women's Team ahead of the World Cup, shifting cultural attention from the men's game and positioning these players as icons in their own right.

The Approach

Sofia partnered with Nike to build a campaign that centred the players—Giulia Gwinn, Klara Bühl, and Selina Cerci—as cultural figures rewriting the rules of football. The strategic frame was about inspiration and name recognition: making the next generation know who these players are.

The Outcome

The campaign successfully shifted the narrative around women's football in the German market and aligned Nike with a progressive, culture-forward position in sport.
