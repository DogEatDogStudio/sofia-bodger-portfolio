Audi Synchronised Swimming

Audi Campaign

Campaign to communicate that Audi's smaller models carry the same DNA and performance credentials as flagship vehicles—without positioning the A1 as a compromise.

The Insight

There is no greater demonstration of two entities matching each other perfectly than synchronised swimmers. The campaign swapped swimmers for an Audi A7 and an A1, performing mirror-image choreography in a competition pool.

The Outcome

The Audi Synchronised Swimming film delivered a distinctive, highly memorable creative execution that cut through category advertising norms and successfully reframed the A1 as a deliberate choice rather than an entry-level compromise.
