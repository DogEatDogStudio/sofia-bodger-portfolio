FAQ
Common Questions
What types of brands do you work with? +

I work with global brands (Nike, Beats By Dre, H&M) as well as ambitious startups and scale-ups looking to connect with culture. My sweet spot is brands targeting Gen Z and millennial audiences, but the principles of cultural intelligence apply to any audience.

How long does a typical project take? +

It varies by scope. A brand sprint workshop can be 1–2 days. A full brand strategy project typically runs 6–12 weeks. Campaign development can be 8–16 weeks depending on complexity. I'll give you a clear timeline after our initial conversation.

Do you work remotely or only in Amsterdam? +

I'm based in Amsterdam but work globally. Most of my projects are hybrid — remote collaboration with in-person workshops or presentations as needed. I've worked with teams across Europe, the US, and beyond.

What's your approach to pricing? +

I offer project-based pricing, retainer arrangements, and workshop packages. After our initial call, I'll put together a tailored proposal based on your specific needs. I'm transparent about costs and what's included.

Can you work with our existing agency or in-house team? +

Absolutely. I often work alongside existing agencies and in-house teams as a strategic partner — bringing an outside perspective and specialist expertise in cultural intelligence and purpose-driven strategy. I've collaborated with teams at BBH, AnalogFolk, and many others.
