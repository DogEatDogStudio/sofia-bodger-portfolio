Nike Mind Sets

Nike Platform

A 365-day global platform leveraging the healing power of movement with a critical reframe—shifting the focus from 'doing' to 'feeling'.

The Approach

Sofia developed the strategy for Nike Mind Sets: a programme that rolled out across the Nike app, NRC, NTC, email, .com, social handles, and athlete channels. The strategy grounded mental wellbeing within Nike's existing movement ecosystem rather than launching a separate vertical.

The Outcome

Nike Mind Sets became a sustained, always-on programme that meaningfully extended Nike's cultural relevance into the mental health space—without feeling performative.
