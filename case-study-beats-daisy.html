Beats Daisy

Beats By Dre Digital Campaign

Beats' first campaign on TikTok establishing genuine cultural credibility with a young, creator-led audience using the launch of Powerbeats Pro.

The Approach

Sofia developed the strategy for the #BeatsDaisyChallenge: a campaign built around Ashnikko's new single 'Daisy'. Rather than a traditional advertising approach, the campaign invited audiences to become co-creators of Ashnikko's music video. Over four weeks, participants were challenged to create colour-themed TikToks inspired by the Powerbeats Pro.

The Outcome

8.1 billion views in the first month. 2.2 million video creations in 21 days. 600,000 unique creators uploading in the first week. 946 million engagements. Covered by Campaign, NME, Music Week, Adweek, The Verge, and others.
