About

Strategic Vision
for Cultural Impact

I help brands understand and connect with youth culture — translating cultural insights into strategy that drives real business results.

With over 12 years of experience leading brand strategy for global agencies and brands across Europe and North America, I've developed a deep understanding of how cultural movements shape consumer behavior and brand perception.

My approach combines rigorous cultural research with strategic positioning to help brands authentically engage with the audiences that matter most. Whether it's music, fashion, sport, or entertainment — I decode youth culture to build brands that resonate.

Previously at Wieden+Kennedy, BBH, and Mother, I've led strategy for Nike, Adidas, Spotify, Converse, and numerous emerging culture-first brands. Now based in New York, I'm focused on helping US brands tap into the cultural currents shaping consumer behavior.
