Introduction

Strategy Led by Empathy & Cultural Intelligence

Hi, I'm Sofia Bodger — a Senior Strategy Director based in Amsterdam with over 8 years of experience at some of the world's most forward-thinking agencies and brands.

I've led strategy at BBH, AnalogFolk, REVOLT, and Highsnobiety, working with global brands including Nike, Foot Locker, Beats By Dre, Johnnie Walker, H&M, Audi, and Tommy Hilfiger.

My approach integrates purpose and brand action — understanding behavior, culture, and human connection to create strategies that drive meaningful change and real business impact.
