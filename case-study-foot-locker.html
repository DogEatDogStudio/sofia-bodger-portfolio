Foot Locker

Foot Locker Brand Platform

'Shoes Don't Change The World'—a brand platform positioning Foot Locker's $200M pledge to the Black community with cultural credibility, not corporate messaging.

The Approach

Sofia built the brand platform from the insight that shoes don't make the person—the person makes the shoes. The platform centred next-generation stories of progress: young people using culture, creativity, and self-expression to create genuine change.

The Outcome

The platform was adopted as Foot Locker's primary brand positioning across EU markets. Campaign content achieved nearly 10,000 likes per post on Instagram. Sofia presented the strategy at the Campaign Purpose Summit alongside the Foot Locker team.
