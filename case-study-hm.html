H&M Social Relaunch

H&M Social Strategy

Complete repositioning of H&M's social presence to rebuild brand love with a fashion-forward audience. The approach was bold enough that H&M wiped their Instagram entirely—generating organic press coverage before a single new post went live.

The Approach

Sofia led the strategy to relaunch H&M's entire social media presence from the ground up. This involved building a comprehensive social playbook that repositioned H&M's platforms through a culture-first lens, followed by the execution of culture-leading content across Instagram and TikTok.

The Outcome

The relaunch positioned H&M at the heart of culture conversations, driving brand love, earned media, and long-term credibility. The wipe moment alone generated significant organic press from fashion and culture publications.
