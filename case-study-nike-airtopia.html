Nike Airtopia

Nike Digital

Air Max Day activation that spoke authentically to a new generation of kids—going beyond product to create a genuine cultural moment built around creativity and play.

The Approach

Sofia developed the strategy for Nike Airtopia: a magical world of Air floating above Nikeland in Roblox. The concept was built in collaboration with the Nike Play Council—a new generation of kid creatives—who transformed their physical creations into a digital world of wonder.

The Outcome

Nike Airtopia delivered a culturally resonant Air Max Day moment that connected digital and physical creativity, positioned Nike as genuinely youth-forward, and created a platform—the Play Council—with long-term strategic utility.
